# Plans and billing

Inkwell Notes has three plans. The Free plan includes up to 3 notebooks and 100 MB of storage.
The Plus plan costs 6 USD per month and includes unlimited notebooks and 10 GB of storage.
The Team plan costs 10 USD per user per month and adds shared notebooks and admin controls.
Annual billing gives a 20 percent discount on Plus and Team.
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day of each billing period.
